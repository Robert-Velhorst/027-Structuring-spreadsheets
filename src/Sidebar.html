Privacy, recovery, and help

Processing stays inside the active spreadsheet and Apps Script. Hidden backups and user-scoped audit metadata are retained locally; only the newest 10 backups are retained.

If automatic restore fails, unhide the named backup sheet and copy the affected range manually. Never delete a backup until validation succeeds.
